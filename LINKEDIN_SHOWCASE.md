🚀 Excited to launch SkillForge — an enterprise-grade AI Career Acceleration Platform built for software engineers! ⚡

As developers preparing for high-impact engineering roles, our workflow is often fragmented: tracking LeetCode on spreadsheets, managing job pipelines on Notion, rebuilding ATS resumes across multiple tools, and practicing interviews in isolation.
